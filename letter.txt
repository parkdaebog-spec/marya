My Dearest Cassy,

I give you these flowers not because I ask anything of you, but because sometimes the heart carries a love too gentle to demand an answer.

Perhaps there are feelings within you that you are still learning how to name. Perhaps you hesitate, or perhaps your heart needs more time before it can speak. And that is okay. I would never want you to rush toward a feeling simply because mine has already found its words.

I am happy simply knowing that you are here.

Even these flowers, blooming only upon a screen, carry something real from me. Each petal is a quiet little “I’m thinking of you.” A small reminder that somewhere, someone smiles simply because you exist.

I often ask you for pictures, not because I need proof of anything, but because I miss your face. There is something about seeing you, even for a moment, that makes an ordinary day feel softer. Your face has become one of those little things my heart looks for without being told.

And if your days become heavy, if the world becomes too loud, I hope you remember this:

You do not have to give me anything in return.

You do not have to confess what you are not ready to confess.

You do not have to understand your heart today.

Just be yourself, and let your heart move at its own pace.

I will be happy for you, whether your heart eventually finds its way toward mine or simply continues on its own beautiful path.

For now, let me love you quietly.

Let me cheer for you from the corner of your life.

And whenever you send me that little picture of your face, know that somewhere in me, a small garden blooms again.

Thank you for being you, Cassy.
Perhaps that is already more than enough for my heart.
