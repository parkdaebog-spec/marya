To My Dearest Cassy

I picked every flower in this bouquet with you in mind. Each one carries a piece of my heart. Click below to see what bloomed for you.

My Cassy,

I'm sorry if it's been a while since we talked like this. I know life has taken us in different directions, and maybe things between us aren't what they used to be. But some feelings don't disappear just because we stop talking about them.

I wanted to give you these virtual flowers. I know they're only flowers on a screen, and I'm sorry if that's all I can give you for now. But I hope when you see them, you don't just see a picture. I hope you see the thought behind them. A little reminder that, even from a distance, someone still remembers you with warmth.

You once told me you're stuck. Maybe I'm stuck too, in my own way. But I've learned that love isn't always about holding on. Sometimes, it's about caring for someone even when you don't know where the road will lead.

If life gives us another chance, I'll be grateful.

If it doesn't, I'll still be grateful that our paths crossed.

Because some people come into our lives like songs. Even when the music stops, somehow, you still remember the melody.

And maybe this is the universe's way of telling us that we're meant to be apart for now. Maybe we're meant to walk different roads, grow into different people, and find the happiness we're supposed to find.

Even if it's ten years from now, or twenty, I'll still hope you're doing well.

And if somehow, in another lifetime, another universe, our paths cross again...

Find me.

Maybe there, I'll get the chance to hug you again. Maybe there, we won't have to lose each other first to understand what we had.

But for this lifetime, if you're meant to go your way, then I hope you win. I hope life is kind to you. I hope you find someone who makes you feel safe, loved, and understood.

And if you ever need me, Cassy, please remember that I'm only one call away. No matter how much time has passed or how far life takes us, you never have to be afraid to reach out. I won't ask for anything, and I won't expect anything from you. I'll simply be there.

If you ever need someone to listen, to laugh with, or just to remind you that you're not alone, you know where to find me.

And if one day you look back and wonder whether someone ever truly cared about you, I hope you remember me.

Because I did.

I still do.

Maybe love is not always about being together.

Sometimes, love is simply saying, "I'm still here, and I hope you're happy."

And for now, that's enough.

So if you ever find yourself somewhere in another lifetime, another universe, and you feel like you've known me before...

Maybe that's just me, still looking for my Cassy. 🌷
