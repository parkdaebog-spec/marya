Confession of Patience My love, Marya, I'm sorry if, for now, all I can give you are virtual flowers. But I hope that when you see them, you also see the effort, thought, and love I put into making them. I made this because I want you to know just how serious I am about waiting for you. I wanted to be the perfect boyfriend for you. I know I wasn't perfect, and I know there are things I could have done better. But I want to become someone you can always be proud of, someone you can trust, and someone who will always be there to support you. I know you're not ready, and I respect that. I won't pressure you or ask you to choose me before you're ready. If it takes four years for you to finish your studies, then I'll be patient. And during those four years, I'll do my best to make your life worthwhile, to make you feel things you've never felt before, and to give you the kind of love and happiness that I know I can give. I'll support you in my own little ways, even through the smallest things, because sometimes it's the little things that mean the most. I love you like a river finds its way to the sea, naturally and patiently. I love you like a soul finds the moon beautiful despite all its craters. I don't love you because you're perfect. I love you because you're you. Maybe love isn't always about holding on. Sometimes, it's about having the patience to let someone grow while still keeping them close in your heart. So do your best. Knowing that if you win, I'll be there to celebrate your victories with you. And when the rain comes to water the flowers, I don't mind getting wet with you. Whatever happens, you don't have to face it alone. I'll be here through the good days, the difficult days, the moments when you feel like giving up, and the moments when you finally realize how far you've come. I want to see you succeed, even if the road there takes time. And if someday you change your mind, I hope you remember that I'll still be here. Not waiting for you to hurry. Just waiting for the day you might finally be ready. I love you. And for now, these virtual flowers will have to speak for the love I can't quite put into words. ❤️
